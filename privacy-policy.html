Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026.

This tool ("FBCOLL") is a private, internal application built and used solely by RE/MAX Everest for its own bookkeeping. It is not offered, sold, licensed, or made available to any other business or individual, and it is not listed on the QuickBooks App Store.

What data this tool accesses

FBCOLL connects to RE/MAX Everest's own QuickBooks Online company using the QuickBooks Online Accounting API. It reads existing chart-of-accounts and class data, checks for duplicate journal entries, and creates journal entries that record the office's own commission and marketing-package collections.

What data this tool does not do

This tool does not access any other QuickBooks Online company. It does not share, sell, or transmit QuickBooks data to any third party. It does not use the QuickBooks Payments API. Any data it reads or writes stays within RE/MAX Everest's own systems (its QuickBooks Online company and its own local database on its own office computer).

Who can see this policy

This page exists to satisfy QuickBooks' requirement that a production app have a published privacy policy. Since this is a private, single-company tool, the only real "user" of the underlying QuickBooks connection is RE/MAX Everest's own office.

Contact

Questions about this tool: contact RE/MAX Everest directly.
